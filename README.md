I've shipped a production AI assistant that cut ticket-resolution time by **40%**, expanded foundation-model benchmarking coverage by **45%**, and built distributed transformer systems across financial services, technology, e-commerce and agriculture.

- 🏢 Currently **GenAI Engineer / Project Coordinator / Technical Lead** at **Symplore Inc**
- 🌾 **Founder & AI Architect** at **Agrivision AI**, building [YieldAI Global](https://yieldaiglobal.com)
- 🔭 Working on LLM evaluation, agentic orchestration and RAG at production scale
- 🌱 Deep into AI safety, alignment, guardrails and hallucination mitigation
- 🎯 Goal: making generative AI reliable enough to put in front of real users
